ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Document Version: 2.1

1. INTRODUCTION
Acme Corporation values the health and well-being of its employees. We believe that taking time off to rest, recover, and spend time with family is essential for maintaining high performance and morale. This document outlines the various types of leave available to full-time employees, the accrual process, and the procedures for requesting time off.

2. TYPES OF LEAVE
Employees are entitled to several categories of paid leave, including Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), and Earned Leave (EL). Additionally, there are provisions for Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave, and Bereavement Leave. Unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the HR department based on business needs.

3. CASUAL LEAVE (CL)
Every full-time employee is entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year. Casual Leave is intended for short, unplanned absences such as personal errands, family emergencies, or religious observances. CL cannot be accumulated and must be used within the same calendar year. A maximum of 3 days of CL can be taken consecutively. Requests for CL should be submitted at least 48 hours in advance, except in cases of extreme emergency.

4. SICK LEAVE (SL)
Employees are provided with 10 days of Sick Leave per calendar year. This leave is strictly for medical reasons, illness, or recovery from surgery. If an employee is on sick leave for more than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted to the HR portal within 5 working days of returning to the office. Sick leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

5. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid Maternity Leave for the birth of their first two children. For the third child, the leave is 12 weeks. Paternity Leave is granted for 14 days (2 weeks) to male employees following the birth of their child. Employees must inform HR at least 8 weeks prior to the expected date of delivery to ensure a smooth transition of their responsibilities.

6. HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
All leave requests must be submitted through the Acme Employee Portal. Employees should select the appropriate leave type, enter the dates, and provide a brief reason. Once submitted, the request will automatically be routed to the direct manager for approval. The manager must approve or reject the request within 2 business days. If a manager is unavailable, the request is escalated to the department head.

7. UNPLANNED ABSENCES
In the event of an unplanned absence due to illness or emergency, employees must notify their direct manager and the HR department via email as soon as possible, but no later than 10:00 AM on the day of the absence. Failure to do so may result in the day being marked as Loss of Pay (LOP).
